## 04:10 2026-08-15

The floor was reverted before it ran. Five and 1000 ms are constants nobody
measured, which is the objection this project raises against every rule it
refuses. 120 ms is 120 ms however many passes fill it; the rep count is a fact
about the kernel, and it is recorded, not thresholded.

it cannot see between sweeps. Replaced by SPEC §4 rule 13 — three whole sweeps
per published cell, agreement is a value common to all three intervals.
