## 19:45 2026-08-15

Built the runner around the measurement, then used it to answer the question
nobody had asked: **is the harness measuring optimised code?**

`bench/tiers.js` re-runs a published cell's exact shape under `--trace-opt
--trace-deopt`, with the timed region's boundaries marked by a preloaded wrapper
around `process.hrtime.bigint` — the one call every workload makes exactly twice.
That is why it reads the workloads unmodified: a `tiers` mode written into all
thirteen would have been thirteen copies of a timed region that is not the one
being measured.

**The assumption holds.** 280 cell shapes, 1590 function-sides observed running
inside a timed region, 1287 of them on TurboFan when the stopwatch started. OSR
is what does it, exactly as guessed: three warmup passes never approach
`invocation_count_for_turbofan`, and the inner loop does not need them to. A cell
sized at two repetitions is still measuring optimised code.

Two things that were assumed and are not true. Maglev is **off** in this Node
(`default: --no-maglev`), so `invocation_count_for_maglev` — cited in README and
verified by `make v8-check` — describes a tier no measurement here has entered.
And Sparkplug is not traced at all, so `none` means Ignition or Sparkplug and the
diagnostic cannot say which.

What is not clean is in TC-21: four cells enter their region at different tiers
on the two sides, and nine run a deopt storm inside the timed region — eight
asymmetrically, one side storming and the other silent. All nine are at the
largest `n` of their sweep, which is where TC-11's audit found low repetition
counts, found again by a probe that knows nothing about repetition counts.

The runner itself: per-cell selection, resume, an arguable load gate (`nproc-1`,
derived from the pin and written into CLAUDE.md so it can be argued with rather
than discovered in an `if`), the environment in every row, the achieved tier in
every row, progress a reader can follow, and `make bench-all` with a manifest.

Two things it caught immediately. A row measured while checking that a flag
parsed reached `bench/spread.jl` at load 1.91 — reverted, and `--scratch` is why
the next one will not (TC-20). And re-measuring `select` moved a published
number: 2.65-2.73x becomes 2.56-2.87x, the rule holds, and its hand-typed silent
clause does not (TC-23).
