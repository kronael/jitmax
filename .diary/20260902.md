## 09:40 2026-09-02 — TC-134 closed by re-measurement

Quieted the machine (46 containers, 3 units), re-ran `node bench/run.ts
select`: 18 rows, 0 void, 3m19s, `runnable` 0 on every one. Five of six cells
replicate. The two sweeps agree cell for cell — the frozen numbers were right
and could not be shown to be.

## 09:45 2026-09-02 — the cause was in resume, not in derive

`done()` counted a frozen-reading row as a measured run, so select's six cells
could never re-measure themselves. `frozen()` joins `hasReading()` in
bench/env.ts. A row with NO reading predates the mechanism and stays counted;
a row that claims to answer rule 9 with one stale number does not.

## 09:50 2026-09-02 — the silent clause lost a claim

`number` at n=100000 read 0.97, 0.89, 1.03 and rule 13 withdraws it. The clause
said the effect "changes sign with the working set"; there is no sign left to
change. It now states n=10000 (1.10-1.19x, under the broad-warning bar) and
n=100000 (no measurement) separately.

## 10:05 2026-09-02 — the `hasReading` gate was nearly a 689-row deletion

Putting `hasReading` on `derive.current()` broke `make numbers` at the first
citation. The query said why: 707 of 976 r2 rows carry no per-row reading, 689
of them outside select. Reverted to a per-citation gate; logged TC-136 for
sign-off. My pre-compaction count of "exactly 18" was wrong — it grepped
`load1` alone, and `hasReading` needs both fields.
