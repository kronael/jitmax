## 09:45 2026-09-02 — the cause was in resume, not in derive

`done()` counted a frozen-reading row as a measured run, so select's six cells
could never re-measure themselves. `frozen()` joins `hasReading()` in
bench/env.ts. A row with NO reading predates the mechanism and stays counted;
a row that claims to answer rule 9 with one stale number does not.
