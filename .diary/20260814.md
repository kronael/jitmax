## 17:44 2026-08-14

project, and it holds at L1, L2 and L3. The four-shape band replicates the
1.15-1.97x the load-site sweep found, from a kernel that shares no code with it.

## 17:47 2026-08-14

Split the call site into its two halves before believing any of it. K maps with
one shared function keeps the four-map budget (2.14-2.21x at four, 6.92-8.25x at
five). ONE map with K functions has no threshold at all: 7.68-11.93x from the
second target to the sixth, flat. The budget is for the receiver's map; a target
loaded out of a field gets none of it.
